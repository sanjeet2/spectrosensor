How to use — Step by step
Step 1 — Power on ESP32
Plug the ESP32 into a USB power source or laptop using a USB cable.
Step 2 — Connect
On laptop (USB cable): Click USB Cable button → a popup appears → select AS7341_Sensor from the list → click Connect.
On Android phone (Bluetooth): First go to Phone Settings → Bluetooth → pair with AS7341_Sensor. Then come back here and click Bluetooth button.
Step 3 — Start measuring
Place your sample in the cuvette. Click ▶ START. Data appears live every second.
Step 4 — Stop and Save
Click ■ STOP when done. A save dialog appears. Enter a name for your experiment. Click Save to Google Sheets.
You will be asked to sign in to your Google account. The data saves automatically to a new sheet.
Browser requirement: This app requires Google Chrome. It will not work in Firefox, Safari, or Edge.
Sensor settings: ATIME=29 · ASTEP=599 · GAIN=4X · DI water blank at Clear ≈ 9449
